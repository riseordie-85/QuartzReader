# Quartz Reader

One library for ebooks, comics, manga and manhwa, on themed shelves. Built for phones, folding phones
and tablets, and part of the Quartz family of apps.

**[Download on the Quartz site](https://quartz.timcordova.com/reader/)** · Needs Android 13 or newer ·
[What's new](CHANGELOG.md)

## Your library

- **One folder for everything.** Pick a library folder and Quartz Reader sorts it into Books, Comics,
  Manga and Manhwa, with each series in its own folder.
- **Opens what you have.** EPUB, CBZ, CBR, CB7, ZIP and PDF files, and folders of images.
- **Series sorted for you.** Volumes are grouped into series by their file names, like v03, c001 or
  Chapter 45, and the library folder can be tidied to match.
- **Shelves of your own.** Make shelves for anything, in shelf, grid or list view.
- **Find anything.** Files, Library, Search and Stats sit along the bottom. Sort the Library by file name,
  title, author or date.
- **Book details when you want them.** Look up missing descriptions and covers from Open Library,
  Google Books, AniList or Comic Vine, and keep ratings, reviews and every time you've read a book.

## A Home you arrange

- Build Home from sections: Recently Read, your Read List, shelf buttons, shelf rows, goals and reading
  stats, in whatever order you like.
- Hold a section's title, or any empty part of Home, to move, add or remove sections.
- Tap a cover to see the book's details before you open it.
- On a tablet or an unfolded phone, the split view puts your shelf beside large covers and each book's
  details.

## Reading

- **Ebooks:** screen-tuned reading fonts, page settings for one book or every book, search, bookmarks,
  highlights and notes.
- **Comics and manga:** page by page or in two-page spreads, read in the right direction for each type.
- **Manhwa:** one long vertical strip.
- **Share cards:** turn a passage or a page into an image to share.
- **Time left:** how long the chapter and the book will take at your own pace.

## Stats and goals

- Reading time for today, this week and this year, plus streaks and a calendar of reading days.
- Finished books by month, by type and by weekday.
- Goals, achievements, monthly recaps and a Year in Review.
- Reading reminders.
- Bring in your history from a Goodreads export, or from an Xteink e-reader running QuartzInk.

## Sync and backup

- Your reading data is saved as a small file in the library folder, so it's backed up and synced along
  with your books.
- Native CrossPoint and KOReader progress sync. When another device is further along, Quartz Reader
  offers to jump to its place.
- Highlights and notes sync with CrossPoint devices.

## Make it yours

- Every shelf can have its own style: header, title, background, shelves, books, buttons and stats cards.
- Start from Black and White, Books, Comics, Manga, Manhwa and more, or build your own in the style
  editor with a live preview.
- Export and import styles, and add your own fonts.

## Privacy

No ads, no analytics and no accounts. Your books, notes and reading history stay on your device and in
your library folder. Quartz Reader only goes online when you look up a book's details or sync with a
server you set up. **Settings > Privacy** lists everything that goes online, and when.
