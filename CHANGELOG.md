# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first release of Quartz Reader: one library for ebooks, comics, manga and manhwa.

### Library
- One library folder, sorted into Books, Comics, Manga and Manhwa, with each series in its own folder.
- Opens EPUB, CBZ, CBR, CB7, ZIP and PDF files, and folders of images.
- Groups volumes into series by their file names, and can tidy the library folder to match.
- Shelves of your own, each with its own style, in shelf, grid or list view.
- Files, Library, Search and Stats along the bottom. The Library sorts by file name, title, author or date.
- Book details, ratings, reviews and every time you've read a book.
- Looks up missing details and covers from Open Library, Google Books, AniList or Comic Vine when you ask.

### Home
- Build Home from sections: Recently Read, Read List, shelf buttons, shelf rows, goals and reading stats.
- Hold a section's title, or any empty part of Home, to move, add or remove sections.
- One tap on a cover shows the book's details, with Open and Details buttons. This can be turned off.
- On tablets and unfolded phones, the split view shows the shelf beside large covers and their details.

### Reading
- EPUBs with the reading fonts from ebook-fonts, page settings per book or for every book, search,
  bookmarks, highlights and notes.
- Comics and manga page by page, in two-page spreads, or as one long strip for manhwa, read in each type's direction.
- Share a passage or a page as an image card.
- Time left in the chapter and the book, at your own pace.

### Stats
- Reading time for today, this week and this year, streaks, reading days, finished books by month, type and weekday.
- Goals, achievements, monthly recaps and a Year in Review.
- Reading reminders.
- Bring in history from a Goodreads export, or from an Xteink running QuartzInk.

### Sync and backup
- Reading data is kept as a small file in the library folder, so it's backed up and synced with your books.
- Native CrossPoint and KOReader progress sync, with your place offered when another device is further along.
- Highlights and notes sync with CrossPoint devices.

### Styles
- Black and White is the default style, and Books is there for a warmer look.
- A style editor for the header, title, shelves, books, buttons, stats cards and background, with a live preview.
- Export and import styles, and add your own fonts.

### Privacy
- No ads, no analytics and no accounts. Settings > Privacy lists everything that goes online, and when.
